A NOTE FROM THE ARTIST

Hello, I’m Pei.

Lovely to meet you here.

I make pictures that hold a little warmth,
a little curiosity, and a reason to smile.

I’m a freelance illustrator exploring children’s stories, playful stationery, digital watercolour and Chinese cultural motifs. Seasons, small creatures and everyday moments find their way into my sketchbook.

This is a place to share those discoveries, with art lovers, exhibition organisers and anyone who enjoys a small escape into an illustrated world.

Procreate · Photoshop · InDesign · Illustrator
